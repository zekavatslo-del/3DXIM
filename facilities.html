Facilities

Our imaging and data processing facilities provide advanced tools for non-destructive characterization of materials and structures.

Imaging Capabilities

We combine laboratory X-ray imaging, high-resolution computed tomography and advanced image processing to investigate internal structures across multiple length scales.

Micro-XCT

High-resolution laboratory X-ray computed tomography for non-destructive 3D characterization of materials and microstructures.

Laboratory X-ray CT

X-ray computed tomography for larger samples and a wide range of engineering and scientific applications.

Synchrotron Imaging

Access to advanced synchrotron X-ray imaging techniques for high-resolution and phase-sensitive measurements.

Image Processing & Analysis

Advanced segmentation, quantitative image analysis and 3D characterization of complex microstructures.

Research Support

Our imaging and data processing facilities at ZAG are available to support your scientific work, from experimental design and image acquisition to reconstruction, segmentation and quantitative analysis.
