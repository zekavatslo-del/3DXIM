About 3DXIM

3DXIM is the X-ray imaging laboratory of the Slovenian National Building and Civil Engineering Institute (ZAG). 3DXIM brings together advanced imaging techniques and quantitative image analysis to investigate the internal structure of materials across multiple length scales.
